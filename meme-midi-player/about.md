## What it does

- Plays the MIDI files bundled with the player or a `.mid` file you fucking stole from somewhere lol.
- Uses my own custom Classic Meme SoundFont by default, or you YEET a other `.sf2` into it!
- Shows active MIDI channels, preset names, channel waveforms, and all 128 MIDI keys.
- Saves audio settings in your browser!

## Audio controls

Open **Audio Settings** to choose polyphony, sample interpolation, and other audio nerd shit! 🤓 \
Extra reverb is optional and starts at 0%; reverb and chorus data in the MIDI and SoundFont are handled by the synth!

Higher polyphony and smoother interpolation can use more CPU. If playback stutters, then lower the settings bro, or else you're basically fucked cuz of your own potato hardware!

## File support

Meme MIDI Player 2000 only supports regular `.mid` MIDI files and `.sf2` SoundFont files! \
