📖 HOW TO PLAY (LORE DROP)

🎯 THE GOAL

Move ALL 52 cards to the 4 Foundation piles (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣), each built from Ace up to King in the same suit. First player to complete their foundations WINS.

🃏 YOUR BOARD

  • Tableau: 7 columns of cards. Move cards in descending rank, alternating color (e.g. red 7 on black 8).
  • Stock (deck): Click it to draw a card to the Waste pile.
  • Waste pile: Top card is playable. Click it to select.
  • Foundations: Build Ace → King per suit. Click a selected card then click a foundation to move it there.
  • Empty columns: Only Kings can go here.
  • Face-down cards: Click the top face-down card to flip it.

🖱️ CONTROLS

  • Click a face-up card to select it (glows yellow).
  • Click a tableau column to place your selection there (if legal).
  • Click a foundation slot to send your selected card there.
  • Click the Stock to draw. When empty, it cycles the Waste back.
  • Click END TURN to hand control to the CPU.

💻 CPU OPPONENT

The CPU plays on its own board simultaneously. It takes 1–3 moves per turn, has a 18% blunder chance, and occasionally skips foundation plays — so it's beatable! When you click END TURN, the CPU does its thing, then it's your turn again.

🏆 SCORING

  • +10 pts — Card moved to Foundation
  • +5 pts — Card flipped face-up
  • +5 pts — Waste card moved to Tableau
  • Time bonus on win: 700,000 ÷ seconds elapsed
